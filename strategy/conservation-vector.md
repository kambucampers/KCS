# The Conservation Vector — application rules

How the conservation angle is applied across all 250 pages without becoming
duplicate content or an unsubstantiated-claims liability.

Read with [`source-context.md`](./source-context.md) §2a and §5, and
[`topical-map.md`](./topical-map.md) Pillar 11.

---

## The single most important rule

> **Conservation appears on every page as a page-specific consequence — never as
> a repeated block.**

The instinct when applying an angle site-wide is to write one good paragraph
about caring for wildlife and communities and drop it into a template. Do not do
this. On a 250-page site that produces 250 near-identical sections, which:

- adds zero information gain to any page;
- creates a large boilerplate footprint that dilutes every page's main content;
- reads as a mission statement rather than evidence, which is precisely the
  pattern that fails E-E-A-T assessment;
- is unfalsifiable, and therefore persuades nobody.

Instead, each page states the conservation fact **that is true of that page's
subject**. The angle is constant; the content is never repeated.

---

## The four-part test

Before a conservation sentence ships on any page, it must pass all four:

1. **Specific to this page.** Would this sentence be equally true on 30 other
   pages? If yes, it is boilerplate — cut it or make it specific.
2. **Evidenced.** Names a partner, a number, a date, a project, or is supported
   by a photograph of the thing described. No exceptions.
3. **Consequential.** It changes something the reader would otherwise decide or
   assume. If it is decoration, cut it.
4. **Proportionate.** Two to four sentences on a Core commercial page. Pillar 11
   is where the argument is made at length; everywhere else it is a note.

## Prohibited constructions

Never publish:

- "We care about the environment / wildlife / local communities" — unfalsifiable
- "Eco-friendly safari", "eco-camp", "eco-lodge" as self-awarded labels
- "Sustainable" with no referent
- "Carbon neutral" or any offset claim without a named scheme and methodology
- "We give back to the community" without the project, the amount and the date
- Comparative claims against unnamed competitors ("greener than other operators")
- Any claim the operations team cannot evidence on request within a day

Kenya's plastic-bag prohibition and similar statutory requirements are **legal
compliance, not achievement**. State them as information for the traveller.
Presenting a legal obligation as a voluntary environmental credential is a
recognised greenwashing pattern and is treated as such by UK and EU regulators.

## Where the argument is allowed to be made at length

Only three pages carry the full case. Everywhere else defers to them:

- `/conservation/why-tented-camps-have-a-lower-footprint/`
- `/conservation/shared-vehicles-and-wildlife-pressure/`
- `/conservation/where-your-money-goes/`

Every page-level conservation note links to whichever of these three is
relevant. That produces a genuine internal link structure around the vector
instead of a repeated block, and it concentrates the topical signal on three
pages that can actually rank for it.

## Evidence register — required before Pillar 11 ships

Collect and keep current. Pillar 11 cannot be written from any of these being
unknown:

- [ ] Conservancy or landowner agreements: counterparty, fee basis, current rate
- [ ] Park and reserve fees paid, by park, current year
- [ ] Staff headcount, roles, and how many are from communities adjacent to the
      areas operated in
- [ ] Local supplier list: produce, water, fuel, laundry, crafts
- [ ] Camp utilities: solar capacity, generator hours, water source and volume,
      waste and greywater handling
- [ ] Fleet: vehicles, typical occupancy, fuel consumption
- [ ] Named projects supported: project, counterparty, amount, date, outcome
- [ ] Any certification or membership held, with membership number and expiry
- [ ] Photographs of every item above

Anything on this list that cannot be evidenced does not appear on the site.
An empty row here is not a gap to be written around — it is a page that does
not get published until the row is filled.

---

## Review cadence

The evidence register and every page carrying a number are reviewed
**quarterly**. `/conservation/impact-report/` is republished **annually with a
visible date**. An undated impact report ages into a liability, and a stale
figure on a conservation page is worse than no figure.
