10-Day Kenya Safari Itinerary: Amboseli, Naivasha and Masai Mara

Kenya travel planning / Research draft dated 15 September 2026

Fees, travel times and sources checked 15 September 2026

Our recommended 10-day Kenya safari itinerary combines Nairobi, two nights in Amboseli, two nights at Lake Naivasha and three nights in the Masai Mara, followed by a final Nairobi night before departure. This route suits first-time visitors who want contrasting wildlife settings, an activity day outside the safari vehicle and time to settle into each destination.

The overnight allocation is 2 nights in Nairobi, 2 in Amboseli, 2 in Naivasha and 3 in the Mara: nine nights in total.

This plan includes your international arrival and departure days. It is not ten full days inside national parks, and it does not require an immediate long-distance transfer after landing.

The itinerary below is a planning recommendation. Your confirmed safari should identify the accommodation, vehicle, meals, admission charges and activities actually booked.

This version gives up the Naivasha activity day. Day 5 becomes a second transfer day in a row, so it suits groups comfortable with consecutive road days. The day-by-day notes below follow the balanced route; Days 6–8 there apply to Days 5–7 here.

How much actual safari time does this route provide?

This itinerary protects three full wildlife-viewing days and one full Naivasha activity day. The complete wildlife days are Day 3 in Amboseli and Days 7–8 in the Mara. Days 2 and 6 can provide additional viewing, but their afternoon plans depend on arrival time, accommodation location and admission arrangements.

That distinction makes quotations easier to compare. A ten-day itinerary with more destinations may provide less uninterrupted viewing time, even when its list of parks is longer.

For a more wildlife-intensive version, shorten Naivasha to one night and increase the Mara to four. That change creates an additional full Mara day while keeping the holiday at ten days and nine nights.

Why combine Amboseli, Naivasha and the Masai Mara?

The three destinations contribute different experiences rather than repeating the same kind of safari day.

Amboseli’s elephants, marshes and open landscapes provide one setting. The Mara contributes extensive grassland wildlife viewing. Naivasha introduces a different pace through selected walking, cycling or lake activities. These are complementary experiences, not a claim that every visitor needs all three. KWS: Amboseli National Park

What do traveller accounts add to the route choice?

First-person reports help explain which experiences mattered, but they do not establish a universal ranking.

In her published ten-day trip account, Kelly Chen described the Naivasha boat-and-walk combination as a welcome contrast to game drives. Her Amboseli experience also illustrated why repeated opportunities matter: the mountain view she wanted did not materialise on the first outing, but conditions improved later. xo, Kelly Chen

Our planning conclusion is to preserve variety and repeat opportunities. Do not make the success of Amboseli depend on one clear photograph, or reduce Naivasha to a hurried stop merely because it appears on other itineraries.

This guide combines those qualitative observations with official destination information and published route references. It does not present selected reviews as a statistical satisfaction survey.

Day 01 / Arrival

Arrive in Nairobi and prepare for the safari

Use your first day for arrival, recovery and a clear departure briefing rather than committing to a tightly timed sightseeing programme.

Arrange an airport transfer to your Nairobi hotel. Choose the hotel together with the safari operator, considering your arrival time and the following morning’s departure.

Before the safari begins, confirm the pickup time, luggage arrangement, driver-guide contact and any outstanding dietary or room requirements. Keep essential medication, identification, travel documents and valuables accessible.

Should you visit Nairobi attractions on arrival day?

An early arrival may leave time for an activity, but do not treat an uncertain flight arrival as a confirmed attraction booking.

The Sheldrick Wildlife Trust’s Nairobi Nursery, for example, admits pre-booked visitors during its fixed 11 a.m.–noon public visiting hour. Separate Nairobi National Park admission is also required. The visit therefore needs deliberate scheduling rather than an improvised stop after immigration. Sheldrick Wildlife Trust

Day 02 / Transfer and possible viewing

Nairobi to Amboseli National Park

Travel to Amboseli in the morning and make any afternoon game drive conditional on your actual arrival.

Your accommodation determines the most appropriate approach and gate. A camp outside the park and a lodge inside it can produce different access requirements, even when both are advertised as “Amboseli accommodation”.

KWS identifies several entrances, including Iremito, Kimana and Meshanani. The correct gate should follow the hotel and route arrangement, not a generic itinerary copied between properties. KWS: Amboseli entrances

After arrival, have lunch, settle into your room and meet your driver-guide for the afternoon plan.

What should the first Amboseli drive focus on?

Use the first outing as an introduction to the landscape and its wildlife. Avoid turning the afternoon into a race to see every named feature.

Discuss your interests with your driver-guide: elephants, birds, photography or a broader introduction to the ecosystem. A clear preference helps them choose how to use the available time.

Day 03 / Full wildlife-viewing day

A full day exploring Amboseli

Keep Day 3 for Amboseli rather than using the morning for another destination transfer.

Amboseli combines open plains with marshes and other wetland habitats. Elephants are a major attraction, while Observation Hill provides an established viewpoint over the surrounding landscape. KWS: Amboseli landscapes

Is a clear Kilimanjaro view guaranteed?

No. Treat a clear mountain view as an opportunity, not an inclusion. Allowing more than one outing gives you another chance under different conditions, but it cannot remove cloud.

The better reason to stay in Amboseli is the whole experience: wildlife, changing light and the contrast between wetland and open country. A single photograph should not determine whether the visit feels worthwhile.

Day 04 / Substantial road transfer

Amboseli to Lake Naivasha

Reserve Day 4 for the journey to Naivasha. This is the itinerary’s main cross-country transfer.

A published Roadtrip Africa route gives approximately eight hours for Naivasha to Amboseli, using the Nairobi bypass and Emali corridor. That is a useful warning about the scale of the journey. For the reverse direction in this itinerary, plan a similarly substantial travel day; it is not a measured hotel-to-hotel guarantee. Roadtrip Africa

Leave after breakfast, plan sensible breaks and keep the evening uncomplicated. A meal and time to relax are more realistic than a prepaid late-afternoon excursion that depends on every road section running smoothly.

Why stay two nights in Naivasha?

The second night creates a complete activity day after the long transfer. With only one night, an arrival delay can remove most of the time you intended to spend there.

One night still works as a transit stop, but it serves a different purpose. Choose that version when you would rather allocate the extra day to the Mara.

Day 06 / Transfer and possible viewing

Naivasha to the Masai Mara

Travel towards the Mara through the appropriate Narok connection, then choose an arrival plan that fits your camp location.

For reference, Roadtrip Africa publishes approximately 4½ hours between Sekenani Gate and Naivasha. The reverse journey and the final drive to your accommodation require their own allowance. A camp near Sekenani and a camp in another Mara sector do not have identical arrival times. Roadtrip Africa

Should you take an afternoon game drive?

Choose an arrival-day drive when you can reach camp, complete the necessary formalities and still have a worthwhile viewing window.

Otherwise, use the afternoon for rest or a separately arranged local activity. An additional reserve-entry day can affect the price, so the decision belongs in the quotation rather than being assumed after arrival.

Where does Kambu Mara Camp fit?

Kambu Mara Camp is Kambu’s own accommodation base, about 9 km before Sekenani Gate on the road from Narok. It is outside the National Reserve, so the stay and Reserve visits are separate parts of the itinerary. That location can suit a Sekenani-based Mara plan, but it should not be described as accommodation inside the Reserve.

Day 07 / Full wildlife-viewing day

Your first full Masai Mara game-drive day

Keep Day 7 free from inter-destination travel and use it for a properly planned Mara outing.

The Mara’s open landscapes support a broad wildlife-viewing experience, including its well-known big-cat interest. The Kenya Tourism Board also identifies ballooning among the destination’s activities, although a balloon flight is a separately arranged experience rather than a normal game-drive inclusion. Magical Kenya

Discuss the day’s focus before departure. Your driver-guide can then plan around your interests, the camp’s position, permitted access and current conditions.

How should the game-drive route be selected?

Choose a workable circuit, not a list of every famous Mara location.

For a Sekenani-based stay, your driver-guide might plan through appropriate eastern and central viewing areas. A longer journey towards the Mara River should be included only when the travel time supports the day’s purpose.

A river-focused itinerary and a patient photography itinerary can use the same hours very differently. Explain which matters more to your group.

Packed lunch or a return to camp?

A packed lunch can avoid a compulsory midday return. A camp lunch can provide a useful break. Neither is automatically better: compare the accommodation location, admission conditions and your preferred pace.

From Kambu Mara Camp, near Sekenani

At Kambu Campers we route Mara days from our own camp, and our Mara driver-guides are based there rather than in Nairobi. The camp sits about 9 km before Sekenani Gate, so splitting a full day into separate morning and afternoon drives can cost you up to an hour in transport if you return to camp in between.

That hour is the real price of a camp lunch. We cannot promise what it would have turned into, because some afternoons are quiet wherever you spend them.

Day 08 / Full wildlife-viewing day

A second full Mara day

Use the second Mara day to deepen the experience, not simply repeat the previous day’s checklist.

You might spend longer at promising sightings, give more attention to birds and smaller animals, or explore a different permitted area. The extra day also creates flexibility when the previous outing did not match your expectations.

Can you plan around the Great Migration?

Yes, but do not build the holiday around a guaranteed river crossing. The migration involves changing animal movements and conditions; even a well-positioned visit cannot schedule a crossing for your vehicle. Mara Conservancy

Your driver-guide should explain the practical trade-off between waiting in a migration area and using the day for wider wildlife viewing.

Can you add a balloon safari?

A balloon flight can fit this morning when the operator, pickup and subsequent transfers are confirmed. It replaces part of the standard morning programme; it does not create additional hours.

Ask how you rejoin your game-drive vehicle and which meals or transfers the balloon arrangement includes.

Day 09 / Return and departure buffer

Return from the Mara to Nairobi

Return to Nairobi on Day 9 rather than making your international departure depend on a same-day long road journey.

Kambu’s published Mara access guidance uses approximately five to six hours for the Nairobi road connection. Your actual camp, departure point, stops and Nairobi destination change the complete journey. Kambu Campers: Masai Mara access

Is a morning game drive included before departure?

Not in this recommended base schedule. Add one only after reviewing the extra admission or operating costs and the later Nairobi arrival.

The final Nairobi night creates a buffer. It also prevents a promising morning sighting from becoming a conflict between wildlife viewing and an airport deadline.

A flight return is another option, but compare the complete airport and hotel connections, not the airborne duration alone.

Day 10 / Departure

For an early departure, the last Nairobi night has already done its job: you are no longer travelling from a distant safari camp.

Does a tented safari require camping equipment?

Not necessarily. A furnished tent and a campsite pitch are different accommodation products. Confirm the beds, bedding, bathroom and charging arrangements instead of assuming that the word “tent” means you must bring camping equipment.

Kambu Mara Camp, for example, has furnished tent accommodation; the photographs below show one of its tents. A bring-your-own-tent campsite arrangement is a separate option.

For families or friends, specify bed sharing versus separate beds. The number of guests a room can accommodate does not tell you how comfortably its sleeping arrangement fits your party.

Choose the operating format according to flexibility, confirmed departure arrangements and the time you want to spend on the road.

What changes when you add a safari flight?

Check baggage before choosing your luggage. Safarilink publishes a standard allowance of 15 kilograms including hand baggage, with soft bags required and different allowances on specified routes. What should the meal plan say?

Ask for a meal-by-meal schedule, especially on transfer days. Confirm whether Day 4 includes a road lunch, whether Days 7–8 include packed lunches, and what is provided during the Nairobi stays.

Dietary requirements should be agreed before arrival. A general description such as “meals included” does not explain every transfer-day arrangement or extra drink.

Is this itinerary suitable for families and photographers?

Yes, when the room, vehicle and activity arrangements match the group, not simply because the itinerary has a suitable duration.

This route suits you if

  • it is your first Kenya safari and you want contrasting settings rather than one park
  • you would value one day on foot, on a bike or on the water
  • you prefer two- and three-night stays to packing every morning
  • your flights leave room for a Nairobi night at each end

Choose a different plan if

  • wildlife time matters most: take four Mara nights instead
  • an eight-hour road day is unsuitable for your group: omit Amboseli
  • you want a beach stay in the same ten days
  • you want Kenya and Tanzania together: that needs a separate plan

For families, discuss children’s ages, separate beds, seating needs, meal timing and tolerance for the Day 4 transfer. A private arrangement may make agreed breaks easier to manage, but it does not shorten the distance.

For photographers, discuss passenger numbers, camera space and the ability to spend time at a sighting. The wildlife-intensive four-night Mara variation may be more useful than adding another park.

For visitors needing mobility support, confirm the actual vehicle access, bathroom arrangements and activity terrain. Do not infer accessibility from a property’s general comfort category.

What should you pack and confirm before travelling?

Pack for repeated vehicle days, early starts and the activities you have actually selected.

  • Clothing and activity needs: Bring comfortable layers, sun protection and suitable footwear. Include walking or cycling clothing only when those activities are planned.
  • Travel essentials: Keep identification, personal medication, booking information and essential electronics accessible.
  • Luggage and equipment: Check the vehicle’s storage arrangement and any flight baggage limit before choosing bags or carrying substantial camera equipment.

KWS’s visitor guidance also emphasises appropriate clothing, drinking water and adherence to park instructions. On game drives, remain in permitted areas, avoid feeding wildlife and use designated places for leaving the vehicle. KWS visitor guidance

For cultural activities, ask who hosts the visit, what the experience involves and what photography expectations apply. For boat and walking excursions, follow the responsible operator’s briefing rather than treating proximity to wildlife as permission to approach.

Frequently asked questions

Is ten days enough for a first Kenya safari?

Ten days is enough for a focused, varied first safari when the route limits unnecessary transfers. This recommendation uses three main safari regions and nine nights, including arrival and departure buffers. It is not intended to cover every major Kenyan destination.

How many nights does a ten-day itinerary include?

This ten-day itinerary includes nine nights. Day 1 is the arrival day and Day 10 is departure. Check whether other quotations count an earlier Nairobi night or an overnight flight outside their advertised duration.

Can I leave for Amboseli on the day I land?

You can when your flight lands early, but this plan deliberately does not depend on it. The first Nairobi night absorbs a late or delayed arrival, so the Amboseli road journey starts the next morning with luggage and pickup already settled. If you remove that night, confirm your flight time before the operator fixes the Day 2 schedule.

Will I see the Big Five?

No itinerary can guarantee all five species. The destinations, time available and daily conditions affect the opportunities. SafariBookings’ destination guidance likewise cautions against treating Big Five sightings as assured. SafariBookings.com

Why does the plan include two Nairobi nights?

The first supports arrival and the second separates the Mara return from the international flight. Remove either only after checking the actual flight and transfer schedule. Those nights are logistical choices, not mandatory stays for every visitor.

Should I visit Amboseli before or after the Mara?

Either order can work. This version places Amboseli first and the longer Mara stay later. Reversing the route may suit different accommodation availability or an onward coast connection, but the long Amboseli–Naivasha journey still needs its own allowance.

Can I add a conservancy night drive?

Only through an appropriate, separately confirmed arrangement. A National Reserve visit and a named conservancy activity are not interchangeable inclusions. The quotation should identify the area, responsible provider, access conditions and any additional cost.

Can this route include both Kenya and Tanzania?

It should not be presented as the same itinerary with an extra country added. A Kenya–Tanzania trip needs a different allocation of nights, border or flight arrangements and supplier responsibilities.
